## 三、Ed Zitron 长文：风险投资正在杀死自己（付费墙，抓取开头）

**Premium: Venture Capital Is Killing Itself** — Ed Zitron，2026-10-16

Zitron 这篇长文追溯了美国风险投资体系崩坏的历史根源——**2012 年 JOBS 法案**：

- **1964 年规则（Section 12(g))**：一家公司超过 500 名股东 + 资产超 $10M，就必须像上市公司一样披露财报。这条规则**倒逼 Facebook 和 Google 上市**——2011 年 Facebook 明确表示不想上市，但因为股东数即将破 500，被迫在 2012 年 5 月 IPO。
- **JOBS 法案（2012-04-05，奥巴马签署）**：把股东上限从 500 提高到 2000，且**员工持股不再计入**。这一刀切断了"被迫上市"的机制，让科技公司可以无限期保持私有化。
- **后果**：公司可以在没有公众监督的情况下野蛮生长，估值被 VC 不断推高，直到 IPO 时以虚高价格卖给散户。Zitron 认为这 14 年来**每一次科技 IPO 都在对散户投资者进行合法收割**。

fact we discovered during the first heavy rainfall after moving in,
when the water rose to shin-height (!!!). But the weirdest and most
nerve-wracking mysteries were the "structural" members of dubious
quality, placement and utility. Some of them only seemed to be
holding stuff up, but on closer inspection turned out to be
free-floating 2x4s, not anchored to anything. On more than one
occasion, our builders have moved jacks into position and removed
those random uprights, only to discover that they weren't holding
anything up. Say what you will about this method, but it is
decisive: if you remove a vertical member and nothing falls down,
you can be pretty sure that wasn't a load-bearing member.

(This is a metaphor about AI.)

You can be forgiven for rolling your eyes when the subject of
"digital sovereignty" comes up. Rhetorically, "digital sovereignty"
is a coded way for a politician to say, "We should build a fuckton
of AI data-centers while bypassing any environmental review." But
AI has nothing to do with digital sovereignty. It is not
load-bearing. All it takes to understand this is a little
thought-experiment. Imagine that we removed all the chatbots from
Canada, or the UK, or the EU, or the USA. What would fall down?
Nothing important. Every public administrative body – national,
regional and local – would continue to function. Every firm – large
and small – would continue to trade. Households would be unscathed,
save for their youngest members, who would suffer the indignity and
tedium of having to do their own homework again.

Which is not to say that "digital sovereignty" is a nothingburger.
The walls of our nations are filled with load-bearing digital members.
It's just that these are the most boring, regular-degular-ass
technologies imaginable. Like Microsoft Office 365. Take away M365
and every ministry collapses, every firm shutters, and many if not
most households will be in serious trouble.

And here's the thing: Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella paid $1m out of
his own pocket to join the murderer's row of tech CEOs who sat
behind Trump on the inaugural dais. Microsoft donated $10 million
to Trump's Epstein Memorial Ballroom. Microsoft provides the
back-end administrative infrastructure for Trump's racist pogroms
(take away M365 and ICE would collapse). The CEO of Microsoft France
admitted that if Trump ordered the company to spy on French citizens'
data (or the data of the French government), his company would
comply, in secret. And here's the kicker: Microsoft has terminated
the M365 and Outlook access of international public officials whose
official acts made Trump angry, starting with the Chief Prosecutor
of the International Criminal Court, who lost his accounts after he
swore out the genocide warrant for Netanyahu.

This is a white-hot, 278-alarm digital sovereignty dumpster-fire.
M365 – as well as cloud software from Oracle, Google, Apple, Amazon
and other US tech giants that have fused themselves with the Trump
regime in exchange for tax breaks and antitrust forbearance – is
load-bearing, and worse, those 2x4s are filled with termites and
dry-rot. Digital sovereignty is as real as real can be. It just has
nothing to do with AI. AI isn't load-bearing. Boring-ass tech
monopolist cloud software is…and we can not trust it.
