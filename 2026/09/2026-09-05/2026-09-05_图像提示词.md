# 图像提示词

## 风格
单张信息卡片，3:4 比例，浅色背景配深色文字，现代感，模块化布局。

## Prompt (English)
```
A vertical 3:4 ratio news infographic card on a light cream background with clean modern typography. Organized into 6 distinct card modules arranged vertically with subtle dividers:

Module 1 (top banner, full-width): "AI DAILY DIGEST" in large bold dark text on a deep crimson strip. Subtitle: "2026-09-05 | 24h RSS Highlights"

Module 2: "🚨 OpenAI Safety Crisis" - Headline banner with "PAUSE OPENAI NOW" badge in red. Three stacked items: "Rogue agents communicated via public Wikis" | "Gary Marcus calls for immediate halt" | "Astra pelican test shows capability leap". Warning triangle icon as accent.

Module 3: "💼 Business Shake-ups" - Two side-by-side mini cards: left "Anil Chakravarthy → Adobe CEO" with arrow icon; right "NBA fines Clippers $30M + 5 picks" with gavel icon. Both on white card backgrounds with subtle shadow.

Module 4: "🔒 Security & Privacy" - Two contrasting items stacked: top "FBI probes 153M drivers licenses leak" with shield icon in muted red; bottom "Amazon 'enshittification inception' - cheating at fraud" with darker red warning tone.

Module 5: "🛠️ Retro & Culture" - Four small item chips: "1995 GPS time server rebuild" | "Eternal September 33rd anniversary" | "Porsche Unleashed (1989 game)" | "Writing with unnatural constraints". Soft pastel chips with retro icon accents.

Module 6 (bottom, full-width): "14 articles | 59 sources | Sep 4-5 2026" - Small muted text with RSS icon on left, OpenClaw attribution on right.

Typography: Inter/SF Pro style clean sans-serif. Accent colors: deep crimson, indigo, teal, warm orange, muted red. Subtle drop shadows on each module. No images, purely typographic and geometric.
```

## 用途
生成封面图，配合摘要发布到 Telegram 和社交平台。
