# 图像提示词

## 风格
单张信息卡片，3:4 比例，浅色背景配深色文字，现代感，模块化布局。

## Prompt (English)
```
A vertical 3:4 ratio news infographic card on a light cream background with clean modern typography. Organized into 6 distinct card modules arranged vertically with subtle dividers:

Module 1 (top banner, full-width): "AI DAILY DIGEST" in large bold dark text on a deep indigo strip. Subtitle: "2026-09-04 | 24h RSS Highlights"

Module 2: "🤖 GPT-6 Astra Released" - Key stats displayed as mini data cards: ARC-AGI 3: 99.9%, ExploitBench: 100%, API: $10/M in. Small label: "OpenAI's Fable competitor with symbolic world models"

Module 3: "⚖️ ASI Ban Bill Proposed" - Two contrasting boxes: Sanders-Casar bill (20yr prison penalty) vs Marcus counter-proposal (conditional approval framework). Dark red vs green accent.

Module 4: "🍎 Apple Updates" - Two items: Phil Schiller steps down from App Store role | iOS 27 iPhone Handoff: shared phone number between two devices. Clean icon bullets.

Module 5: "🌐 Post-Trump Internet" - Doctorow's warning visualized as a globe with "US Tech Shutdowns" warning icon, key quote: "The only way out: open, auditable, sovereign digital public goods"

Module 6 (bottom, full-width): "14 articles | 59 sources | Sep 3-4 2026" - Small muted text with RSS icon.

Typography: Inter/SF Pro style clean sans-serif. Accent colors: indigo, coral red, teal, warm orange. Subtle drop shadows on each module. No images, purely typographic and geometric.
```

## 用途
生成封面图，配合摘要发布到 Telegram 和社交平台。
