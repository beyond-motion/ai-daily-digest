### 2. Drew Breunig：免费午餐结束了
**作者**: Drew Breunig（Simon Willison 转引） · **来源**: Simon Willison's Weblog · **时间**: 2026-08-23 19:55

**核心引述**:
> "Prior to Fable, it felt silly to waste too much time improving your coding harness or context strategies. A new model would arrive at the same price (or cheaper!) and paper over most of your problems. But then Fable landed... the cost was so high and Opus was good enough... So we started to think about what work went where."

**这意味着什么**:
- 不再有「换个模型就解决一切」的银弹
- 上下文工程、harness 设计、路由策略重新变得重要
- 任务分层（cheap model 处理 X，expensive model 处理 Y）成为新常态

🔗 [原文](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/23/drew-breunig/)
